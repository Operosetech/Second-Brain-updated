eCommerce

Odoo eCommerce provides an open-source platform for building and managing an online store. It offers a wide range of tools and features to configure products, customize the design, manage ordering and checkout processes, set up delivery methods, handle orders, and analyze performance.

Configuration

Configure products, create product variants and categories, and set up pricing. Use customer accounts to manage access according to your business model (B2B or B2C) and needs.

eCommerce design

Design the product catalog and product pages using design elements, building blocks, and e-commerce-specific options and buttons.

Ordering and checkout

Customize the ordering and checkout process to fit your business needs.

Delivery

Enable and configure delivery methods to provide customers with one or multiple options upon checkout.

Order handling

Handle the complete e-commerce order lifecycle, from sales and abandoned carts to delivery, stock management, returns, and invoicing.

Performance management

Google Merchant Center

Connect Odoo eCommerce to Google Merchant Center and other platforms, such as TikTok, Facebook, or Instagram to showcase products across multiple channels.

Note

Odoo offers a free custom domain name to all Odoo Online databases for one year. Visitors can then access your website with an address such as www.example.com rather than the default example.odoo.com .
